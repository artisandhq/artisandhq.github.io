Privacy policy

This policy covers the mobile apps slide., sudoku. and 2048. (the "Apps") published by artisandhq. The short version: we don't run servers, we don't ask you to make an account, and your game data stays on your device. The Apps show ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to do that. The details are below.

1. Who we are2. Data kept on your device3. Advertising (Google AdMob) 4. In-app purchases5. Fonts6. Your choices 7. Children8. Your rights9. Changes10. Contact

01Who we are

artisandhq is the name under which Richard Firdaus Oeyliawan, an individual developer based in New Taipei City, Taiwan, publishes apps. In this policy "we" and "us" mean artisandhq. For data handled inside the Apps by Google AdMob, Google is an independent controller of that data.

02Data kept on your device

The Apps save the following only in local storage on your phone. We never receive it, and it is not backed up to any server of ours:

  • Game progress and saved games, scores, best times, statistics and daily streaks
  • Settings such as theme, sound and difficulty
  • Which themes you have unlocked and whether you bought "Remove ads"

Photos (slide. only)

slide. lets you turn a photo into a puzzle. If you use this, the app asks for permission to pick a photo from your library. The photo is cut into tiles on your device and is not uploaded, shared or sent to us or anyone else. You can refuse or withdraw photo access at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Deleting an App removes all of this data from your device.

03Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Apps are free and show ads served by Google AdMob: full-screen ads between games and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example for an undo or a hint). To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

DataPurpose
IDFA (only if you allow tracking)Ad delivery, frequency capping, personalised ads
IP address and approximate location derived from itChoosing ads for your region, fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device
Ad interactions (impressions, taps) and diagnosticsMeasurement, analytics, fraud prevention

This data goes directly from your device to Google. We only see aggregated reports. Google's use of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Apps ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. On iPhone, the Apps ask for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised using the IDFA.

If you buy "Remove ads", full-screen ads stop. Rewarded ads remain available only when you choose to watch one.

04In-app purchases

Premium themes and "Remove ads" are sold through the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple under its own privacy policy. We never see your name, payment card or billing details. The App only receives a confirmation that a purchase was made, which it stores on your device so the item stays unlocked.

05Fonts

The Apps' typeface (Manrope) is built into each App. Nothing is downloaded from Google Fonts or any other font service.

06Your choices

  • Consent (EEA/UK/CH): change your choice at any time from Settings → About → Privacy choices inside each App.
  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and switch the App off.
  • All ads: buy "Remove ads" in the App to stop full-screen ads.
  • All data: delete the App.

07Children

The Apps are general-audience games and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an App, contact us and we will help remove it.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you on our side, most requests are best handled through the controls in section 6 or directly with Google at My Ad Center. We will gladly help with any request; write to us at the address below.

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising data shared with Google as described in section 3 may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under some US state laws; you can opt out using the controls in section 6.

09Changes

If we change how the Apps handle data, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
